# A Link-Purpose Review for Outsourced Website Design

Read links with their surrounding heading and, where needed, as a list of links. Turn repeated “learn more” labels into wording that identifies the subject or action.

## Make the decision visible

Do not add descriptive words that promise more than the destination provides. The best label is accurate, brief, and useful when the page is scanned.

## Review the real page

Review text links, cards, buttons, footer links, and links in error messages. Confirm that the destination remains correct after page names or routes change.

## Keep the handoff useful

A link-purpose checklist helps an outsourced team preserve orientation across templates and makes acceptance easier for your content owner.
