# A Social Proof Brief for Outsourced Website Design

Record the source, permission, context, date, and approved wording for each testimonial, client mark, result, or quotation. Say what the evidence does and does not establish.

## Make the decision visible

Do not invent missing details or turn one experience into a universal promise. If a name or result cannot be published, choose a different form of evidence.

## Review the real page

Review the proof beside the claim it supports, then test the mobile layout and alternative text. Keep a clear path to the source or fuller context when one exists.

## Keep the handoff useful

This gives a design partner credible material to work with and gives your company a review trail for every public proof element.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
