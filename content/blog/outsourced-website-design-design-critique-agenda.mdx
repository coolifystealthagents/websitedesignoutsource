# A Focused Design Critique Agenda for Outsourced Website Projects

Design critique works best when reviewers discuss the same question at the same time. An agenda keeps feedback from turning into a long list of personal preferences.

## Confirm the review frame

Open with the route, audience, viewport, and decision under review. State whether the group is assessing structure, visual direction, content, or readiness for the next stage.

## Review hierarchy first

Ask what a visitor notices, understands, and can do next. Resolve major hierarchy and flow questions before discussing small spacing or styling details.

## Separate findings from ideas

Record a required correction, a question, and a possible future enhancement as different categories. This helps the external team know what must change before the next review.

## Close with owners

End with a decision, an owner for each open item, and the evidence needed to close it. Keep the accepted direction visible so later feedback does not reopen settled choices without a reason.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should every stakeholder attend?

Invite people who own the decision or provide necessary subject knowledge. A smaller group with clear authority usually produces more useful feedback.

### How should disagreements be handled?

Return to the page goal, audience, and agreed evidence. If the question remains open, record it as a decision for the named owner rather than forcing a personal preference.
