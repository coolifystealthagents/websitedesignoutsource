# Feedback Triage for an Outsourced Website Design Review

Sort findings by user harm, business relevance, scope impact, and dependency. A missing route or inaccessible control should not compete with a low-impact visual preference.

## Keep context attached

Record the page, component, viewport, evidence, expected result, and decision. This lets an external team resolve a finding without guessing what the reviewer meant.

## Recheck accepted work

After changes, verify the original condition and inspect nearby states. Triage is complete when the evidence supports the disposition, not when a comment is merely marked closed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
