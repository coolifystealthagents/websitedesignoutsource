# A Case Study Page Structure That Keeps Evidence Credible

Describe the starting situation, constraints, decisions, implementation, and observable result. Distinguish client-provided facts from interpretation and avoid filling gaps with invented detail.

## Show the decision trail

Explain why a design direction was chosen and what changed after review. A short before-and-after comparison is useful when it names the condition being compared.

## Review claim strength

Check every outcome statement for its source and scope. If the evidence is qualitative, describe it as qualitative rather than converting it into an unsupported number.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
