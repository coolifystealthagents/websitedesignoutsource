# Plan Service Page Architecture for Outsourced Website Design

Service pages need to explain a real offering without forcing every detail into one crowded route. Architecture decisions made early help an external design team create pages that are easy to understand and maintain.

## Group by visitor questions

List what a visitor needs to know about fit, approach, deliverables, responsibilities, and next steps. Use the questions to choose sections and supporting routes.

## Separate overview from detail

Keep the main page focused on orientation. Move deep explanations, examples, or related topics to pages that can be linked when a visitor needs them.

## Make scope visible

Explain what the service covers and what requires a separate decision. Clear boundaries help visitors and the design team avoid assumptions.

## Review the route as a system

Check links, headings, metadata, calls to action, and related pages together. A service page is successful when the surrounding path supports the same decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should every service have its own page?

Create a distinct page when the audience, questions, or decision meaningfully differ. Combine pages when they repeat the same explanation.

### Who supplies service details?

The company supplies and approves the facts. The outsourced team can organize and present them clearly.
