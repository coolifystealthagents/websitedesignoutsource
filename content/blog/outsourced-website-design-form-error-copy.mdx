# Form Error Copy to Specify in an Outsourced Website Design Brief

Name the field or task, explain the correction in plain language, and keep the entered value when it is safe to do so. Put the message close to the relevant control.

## Make the decision visible

Check errors before submission, after submission, and when a connection or integration fails. These states need different instructions and should not all say “try again.”

## Review the real page

Read the error sequence with a keyboard and a screen reader. The user should reach the message and understand the next step without searching the whole page.

## Keep the handoff useful

Documenting these states gives an outsourced team enough detail to design a complete form rather than a successful-path screenshot.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
