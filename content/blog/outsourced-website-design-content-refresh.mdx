# Planning a Content Refresh for an Outsourced Website

Inventory pages by audience, task, owner, freshness, search intent, and observed problem. Decide whether each page should be retained, clarified, consolidated, redirected, or retired.

## Preserve what works

Compare proposed changes with existing links, metadata, structured content, and known journeys. Keep accurate material where it helps, then focus effort on the gaps that block understanding.

## Define acceptance

For each refreshed page, specify the intended reader, required sections, approved facts, internal links, and review owner. Validate the rendered page as well as the edited source.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
