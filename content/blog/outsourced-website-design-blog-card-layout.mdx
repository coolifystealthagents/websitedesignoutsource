## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the audience, task, page context, approved inputs, constraints, review setup, and acceptance condition. Name one owner for the final decision.

### Is a visual mockup enough?

No. The reviewer should use the interaction and check the states that matter to the visitor, including narrow layouts, keyboard access, errors, and the final content.
