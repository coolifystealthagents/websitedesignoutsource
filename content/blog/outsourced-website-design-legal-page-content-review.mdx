# Content Review for Legal and Policy Pages on Business Websites

Give the design team approved source text, effective dates, jurisdictional context, and the route each document should use. The visual treatment should support reading without changing the legal meaning.

## Preserve document integrity

Do not summarize, merge, or rewrite controlled language inside the design file. Mark links, headings, definitions, and required notices so reviewers can check placement separately from wording.

## Check access and discovery

Make policy pages reachable from the places visitors expect, including relevant forms and the site footer. Confirm the canonical route and link behavior after navigation changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
