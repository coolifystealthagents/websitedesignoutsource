# Use a Content Inventory Workshop Before Website Design

An inventory workshop is useful when a spreadsheet alone cannot settle ownership or page status. Keep the session focused on facts and decisions that affect design.

## Bring the current list

Start with URLs, titles, page types, owners, status, and known dependencies. Mark uncertain entries rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

## Resolve the important questions

Ask which pages must remain, which can be combined, which need new content, and who approves each change. Do not rewrite every page during the workshop.

## Record evidence and next steps

Add analytics, support questions, legal constraints, or search context when they change priority. Give every open item an owner and a date.

## Hand the result to design

Provide the inventory, priority order, route notes, and approved page jobs. The designer can then focus on structure instead of rebuilding project history.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who should lead the workshop?

Someone who can keep the group on decisions and record ownership. That person does not need to own every content area.

### What happens to uncertain pages?

Keep them visible with a named owner. Uncertainty is useful information when setting the design sequence.
