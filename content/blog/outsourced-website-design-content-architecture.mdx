# Content Architecture Decisions to Make Before Outsourced Website Design

List the main visitor questions, then map each question to one page or a clearly named section. Mark which information is shared across pages and which belongs to one context.

## Make the decision visible

Avoid making the navigation mirror your internal departments. Organize around the tasks visitors need to complete and the evidence they need to trust.

## Review the real page

Ask the design team to show the map beside the wireframes. A missing page, duplicated claim, or buried next step is easier to spot before visual polish begins.

## Keep the handoff useful

The result is a usable brief that gives an outsourced team structure to design against while leaving room for the right visual solution.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
