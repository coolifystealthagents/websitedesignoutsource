# Create a Pricing Table Content Brief Before Visual Design

**Published: September 7, 2026**

Pricing tables fail when decoration gets ahead of product facts. Give the design partner one approved source for plan names, prices, billing terms, limits, taxes, trials, renewals, and cancellation language.

## Normalize the comparison

Write each feature in the same grammatical form and define what it includes. Separate true plan differences from explanations that apply to every customer. If a limit depends on usage, geography, or contract terms, state that dependency near the claim.

## Clarify price presentation

Document currency, billing period, equivalent monthly calculations, introductory periods, and what happens afterward. Do not use a crossed-out price without an approved basis. The client commercial owner should approve every figure and qualifier.

## Design for scanning and access

- Use headings and lists that preserve meaning outside a visual grid.
- Keep feature labels visible when comparing narrow columns.
- Avoid conveying inclusion by color or icons alone.
- Define the selected billing period in text.
- Test long plan names, translated labels, and zoom.

## Establish update ownership

Link every displayed value to its owner and source. Add a review date and a fast correction path. Pricing should not be copied into several components without a synchronization plan.
