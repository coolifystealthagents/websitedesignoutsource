# A Practical Homepage Content Sequence for Outsourced Web Design

A useful order is audience and problem, service approach, evidence, scope cues, frequently asked questions, and a clear next step. The sequence can change, but each section should answer a new question.

## Design for scanning

Give each section one job and a descriptive heading. Use short paragraphs, meaningful labels, and supporting links so visitors can skip to the detail they need.

## Check the handoff

Review the sequence in the actual layout at narrow and wide widths. Confirm that the first screen identifies the audience and that important qualifications are not buried below decorative content.

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
