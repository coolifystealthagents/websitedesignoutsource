# Brief an Account Deletion Flow With Clear Consequences

**Published: September 7, 2026**

Account deletion sits at the intersection of product design, security, privacy, support, and data operations. The outsourced team needs approved decisions from those owners before it can design an honest journey.

## Define what deletion means

List the records removed, retained, anonymized, or handled on another schedule. Explain effects on subscriptions, shared work, published material, integrations, exports, and access. Do not let interface copy promise immediate erasure when systems follow an approved retention process.

## Design an informed choice

Show the account identity, important consequences, available export, and whether recovery is possible. Confirmation should be proportionate to risk without creating an unnecessary memory test. Keep cancel and continue actions unambiguous.

## Cover the complete sequence

- Entry from account settings or an approved support route.
- Reauthentication when required by the security owner.
- Dependencies that must be resolved first.
- Pending, completed, failed, and partially completed states.
- Confirmation through a safe channel.
- Sign-out and later attempts to use old credentials.

## Test without harming real accounts

Use synthetic accounts containing representative linked data. Record the request, user-visible state, backend outcome, notification, and audit reference. Route policy and legal questions to qualified client owners.
