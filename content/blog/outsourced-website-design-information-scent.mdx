# Information Scent: A Useful Review Lens for Outsourced Website Design

Write the question in the visitor’s words, then inspect the nearby heading, label, summary, and destination. Each clue should point in the same direction.

## Make the decision visible

Change clever labels that require interpretation to specific language. A short label is useful only when its meaning is obvious in context.

## Review the real page

Test the page with someone who did not write the content. Ask what they expect to find before they click and compare that answer with the actual destination.

## Keep the handoff useful

This gives your design partner a practical way to improve navigation and content hierarchy without relying on personal taste.

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
