# Hand Off Website Copy to an Outsourced Designer

Copy and design meet in the layout. A clean handoff tells the outsourced designer what is approved, what may change, and which words carry a business or audience requirement.

## Label the copy state

Mark headings, body text, labels, metadata, and calls to action as approved, draft, or reference. Include the owner for each open wording decision.

## Show the page structure

Provide the intended order, links, image relationships, and content that must appear together. A document full of paragraphs does not show which text belongs to which page block.

## Include realistic length

Keep long headings and useful error messages in the handoff. Shortened copy can make a layout seem stable until the final content arrives.

## Review meaning after layout

Check that emphasis, order, link context, and labels still say what the writer intended. Ask for a wording decision when layout pressure makes a factual or audience change tempting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the designer edit copy for length?

Only when that responsibility is agreed. The content owner should approve any change to meaning.

### What belongs in a copy handoff?

The approved text, structure, status, links, asset relationships, open questions, and decision owners.
