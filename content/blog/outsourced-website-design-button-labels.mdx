# How to Review Button Labels in an Outsourced Website Design

Name the destination or result in the label. “View the service details” is easier to understand than a generic label because it sets an expectation before the click.

## Make the decision visible

Check each label in its surrounding card, on a narrow screen, and when read out of context. Repeated labels should still be distinguishable when several appear on one page.

## Review the real page

Do not use urgency or invented certainty to make a button sound persuasive. The label should describe the real next step and match the destination page.

## Keep the handoff useful

Include approved labels in the design handoff so the team does not swap precise language for temporary text during layout revisions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
