## Frequently asked questions

### What belongs in the brief?

Include the visitor task, content and evidence constraints, review conditions, and the owner for the decision. Keep assumptions separate from confirmed facts.

### How should an external team be reviewed?

Review the working route and representative states against the brief. Record a specific change and its reason rather than giving only a general visual reaction.
