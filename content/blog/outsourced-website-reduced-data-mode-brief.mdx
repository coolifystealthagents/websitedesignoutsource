## Define the visitor outcome

Reduced-Data Mode Brief for Outsourced Website Teams treats reduced-data behavior as an observable client acceptance question, not a screenshot preference. At checkpoint 1, identify media-heavy routes, the approved requirement, affected route, viewport or input state, expected result, and product owner. Record WD-B-outsourced-website-reduced-data-mode-brief-1 so another reviewer can repeat the decision.

The define the visitor outcome checkpoint runs the network simulation on an assembled route, with checkpoint-specific attention to media-heavy routes and reduced-data behavior. Preserve browser, version, content fixture, result, defect owner, due point, and retest evidence under WD-B-outsourced-website-reduced-data-mode-brief-1.
