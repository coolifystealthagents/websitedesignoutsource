# Choosing Proof Points for an Outsourced Website Service Page

Start with the service promise, then identify what a cautious buyer needs to verify: scope, method, evidence, fit, or next step. Use concrete process descriptions and observable outcomes rather than inflated claims.

## Match proof to doubt

Place each proof point beside the question it answers. A relevant example can clarify fit, while a qualification or working method can explain how the service is delivered.

## Keep claims supportable

Review every statement for source, owner, and time sensitivity. If a result depends on client conditions, describe those conditions instead of presenting the result as universal.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
