# Writing a Website Launch Rollback Plan with Clear Triggers

Define the decision owner, trigger conditions, last known good state, affected routes, communication path, and verification checks. Keep the plan specific enough to use under pressure.

## Separate symptoms from triggers

A broken image may need a quick fix, while a widespread routing or form problem may require rollback. Write the threshold for each class of issue and identify who can declare it.

## Rehearse the evidence

Walk through the plan with the build record and route inventory. Confirm that backups, access, redirects, and post-rollback checks are understood before the launch window.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
