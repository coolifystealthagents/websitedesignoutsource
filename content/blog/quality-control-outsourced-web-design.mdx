# Upholding Standards: Robust Quality Control for Outsourced Web Design

One of the primary concerns when outsourcing is maintaining quality. However, with the right strategies and processes in place, you can ensure that your outsourced web design project meets or even exceeds your expectations.

## Clear Scope and Deliverables

Begin with a meticulously defined project scope, detailed requirements, and clear deliverables. This forms the baseline against which all work will be measured.

- Detailed project brief
- Wireframes and mockups
- Functional specifications
- Acceptance criteria


## Regular Review and Feedback Loops

Implement a system of continuous review and feedback. Schedule regular check-ins and formal review stages to catch issues early and ensure alignment with your vision.

- Milestone reviews
- Iterative feedback
- User acceptance testing (UAT)
- Bug tracking

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should I review progress?

Depending on the project's complexity, daily or weekly check-ins are advisable, with formal reviews at key milestones.

### What if the quality is not up to par?

Address issues immediately, refer back to the initial agreement, and work with the team to implement corrective actions.
