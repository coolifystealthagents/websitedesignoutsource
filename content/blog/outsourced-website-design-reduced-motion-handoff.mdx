# Put Reduced Motion in the Animation Handoff

**Published: September 10, 2026**

An animation specification needs a second column: what happens when a visitor asks the operating system to reduce motion. Removing every transition is not always necessary, but leaving the choice to each developer creates uneven behavior.

## Inventory motion by purpose

List entrance effects, parallax, scrolling, carousels, progress changes, decorative loops, and animated media. Note which motion communicates state and which is decorative. Capture duration, trigger, repetition, and stop controls.

## Design the reduced alternative

Replace large movement with an immediate state change, a short fade, or a static presentation when that keeps the meaning. Do not hide content because its reveal animation is disabled. Give visitors a pause control where motion continues independently.

## Verify the preference

Switch the system preference before loading the page, then inspect every representative component. Check after client-side navigation and after changing the preference while the page is open. Record any animation that remains and why it is necessary.
