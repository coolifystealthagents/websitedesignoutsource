# Audience Segmentation for an Outsourced Website Design Brief

Write three visitor situations, the question each person brings, and the evidence that would help them continue. Give each situation a primary page and a next step.

## Make the decision visible

Keep the segments behavioral. A useful segment describes a job, concern, or decision rather than a vague demographic label.

## Review the real page

Review the first screen, navigation labels, and calls to action against each situation. Remove sections that serve nobody in the approved audience.

## Keep the handoff useful

When outside designers receive these scenarios, they can explain why a page exists and make review comments tied to visitor intent.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
