## Review the working page

Ask the outsourced team to provide the working route or a usable prototype, not only a polished image. Review the main task at a narrow viewport, with keyboard navigation and zoom, and record the exact state that needs changing. Separate a blocking defect from a later enhancement.

## Close with an owned handoff

The final record should name the approved version, source files, content owner, open follow-ups, and the next person responsible for changes. That small amount of context prevents the next update from becoming a reconstruction exercise.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the audience, task, page context, approved inputs, constraints, review setup, and acceptance condition. Name one owner for the final decision.

### Is a visual mockup enough?

No. The reviewer should use the interaction and check the states that matter to the visitor, including narrow layouts, keyboard access, errors, and the final content.
