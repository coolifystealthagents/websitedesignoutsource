# How to Map Content Approvals in an Outsourced Website Project

Separate subject-matter review, legal review, brand review, and final acceptance. One person may perform several reviews, but each decision should still have a named owner and a clear stopping point.

## Define decision states

Use simple states such as draft, review, changes requested, accepted, and superseded. Record the page, version, reviewer, decision date, and reason for any material change.

## Protect page intent

When approved wording changes, check headings, components, metadata, and calls to action together. That prevents a late copy edit from quietly changing the page's purpose.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
