# Briefing Team Bio Pages for an Outsourced Website

Define the visitor question each bio answers, the role context, the approved facts, and the link to the service or work it supports. Keep the format consistent while allowing useful differences.

## Use evidence carefully

Include experience or responsibilities only when they are confirmed and current. Explain how the person's role contributes to the project instead of using vague authority language.

## Respect privacy

Agree what personal details can be public and who approves updates. Avoid publishing contact information or claims that have not been reviewed by the person named.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
