# Prepare a Video Embed Handoff for an Outsourced Website

**Published: September 1, 2026**

Embedded video introduces an external player, network cost, accessibility requirements, and account dependency. A handoff should cover more than the poster image and play button.

## Define content and platform ownership

Record the source file owner, hosting account, embed address, poster treatment, title, transcript, captions, privacy settings, replacement procedure, and expiration risk. Explain whether the player loads immediately or only after visitor action and how that choice affects consent and performance.

## Review the complete playback experience

- Test keyboard access to player controls.
- Verify captions and transcript match the published edit.
- Check narrow screens and full-screen behavior.
- Observe the page when the host is blocked or unavailable.

## Manage risk and ownership

Do not rely on auto-generated captions without review. Avoid autoplay with sound and ensure important information is available outside the video when appropriate.

## Define acceptance

The client should be able to swap or remove the video, update supporting text, and identify the host account owner. Preserve test evidence for accessibility, privacy, and failure behavior.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who should own this record?

A named client owner should approve business rules and access. The outsourced team maintains implementation detail and supplies repeatable evidence.

### When should the team review it?

Review it before implementation, at acceptance, and whenever a related service, policy, account, or production component changes.

### Does a template prove the work is complete?

No. The completed record must match the implemented website and be supported by checks that another reviewer can repeat.
