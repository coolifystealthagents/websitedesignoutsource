# How to Run a Wireframe Review with an Outsourced Website Design Team

Give reviewers the page intent, target visitor, known constraints, and the questions you want answered. Ask them to perform a task before collecting visual reactions.

## Make the decision visible

Capture decisions separately from suggestions. A useful record says what changed, why it changed, who owns the decision, and what still needs evidence.

## Review the real page

Review the empty, error, mobile, and long-content states. A wireframe that works only for perfect sample content is not ready for design completion.

## Keep the handoff useful

This rhythm keeps feedback specific and lets an outsourced team iterate against agreed page behavior instead of chasing disconnected preferences.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
