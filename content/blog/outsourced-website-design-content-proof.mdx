# A Content Proof Checklist for Outsourced Website Design

Read the page in order and compare every claim with the approved source. Confirm headings, labels, links, captions, and the final action rather than checking only the hero section.

## Make the decision visible

Mark facts that need an owner, not just a copy edit. A page can be grammatically clean while still making a claim that nobody has approved.

## Review the real page

Review desktop and mobile layouts because line breaks, hidden tabs, and collapsed sections can change meaning. Save the exact page version that was reviewed.

## Keep the handoff useful

This gives your design partner a clear correction list and gives the company a durable record of what the finished page was meant to communicate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
