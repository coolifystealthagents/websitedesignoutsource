# Content Ownership in an Outsourced Website Project

Content ownership clarifies who supplies, approves, maintains, and retires the words and media that make a website useful.

## Assign responsibility by content type

Name an owner for service descriptions, proof, images, legal text, navigation labels, and reusable components. Avoid assigning ownership only to a department.

## Define approval authority

Record who can approve factual claims, brand language, accessibility wording, and regulated information. Give reviewers a concrete route or component to inspect.

## Plan ongoing stewardship

Document what should be reviewed when a service changes, an image license expires, or a page no longer serves its visitor task. Keep the ownership record close to the relevant route.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why name an individual owner?

An individual owner makes a decision and follow-up request easier to route than an undefined group.

### Does ownership remove review?

No. It makes review and maintenance responsibilities visible so the right person can participate.
