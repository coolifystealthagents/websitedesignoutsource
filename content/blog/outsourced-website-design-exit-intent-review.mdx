# A Careful Exit-Intent Review for Outsourced Website Design

Start by asking why someone may leave: unclear relevance, missing evidence, slow interaction, or a task that cannot be completed. Observe the page before adding another message.

## Make the decision visible

Do not treat every departure as a conversion problem. A visitor may have found the answer, decided to return later, or reached a page that should not interrupt them.

## Review the real page

If an interruption is justified, make its close control clear, keep the message short, and ensure keyboard and mobile users can dismiss it. Review the destination and the language together.

## Keep the handoff useful

This approach gives an outsourced design team a visitor-centered problem to solve instead of asking for a distracting overlay by default.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
