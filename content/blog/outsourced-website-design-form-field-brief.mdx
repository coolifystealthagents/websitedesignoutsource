# A Form Field Brief for Outsourced Website Design

Form design starts with the information a business needs and the effort a visitor can reasonably provide. The outsourced website design team should receive those decisions before styling labels and controls.

## State why each field exists

For every field, record its purpose, required status, acceptable format, owner, and what happens after submission. Remove fields that have no clear use. If a question is optional, explain why it appears at all.

## Describe errors before layout

Provide messages for missing, invalid, and unavailable values. Say whether entered information remains after an error. The design should make the relationship between label, control, help text, and error clear at a narrow width.

## Protect the interaction

Ask for keyboard access, visible focus, readable labels, sensible tab order, and a confirmation state. The outsourced website design team can show the interaction, but the form owner must approve the meaning of consent, privacy, and follow-up text.

## Hand off behavior and source

Return the field map, approved copy, route, destination owner, and tested states with the editable design files. Do not treat a polished static form as proof that the full interaction is ready.


## Make each field earn its place

For every field, record the decision it supports, whether it is required, acceptable examples, and the error a visitor sees after an invalid value. Remove fields that do not change the next step. The outsourced website design team can then design labels, help text, and grouping around a real task instead of copying a generic form pattern.
