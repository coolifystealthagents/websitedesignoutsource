# A Brand Voice Brief for an Outsourced Website Design Project

Describe the audience relationship in plain language, then list three traits the writing should convey and three habits it should avoid. Pair every trait with a short example.

## Make the decision visible

Separate voice from tone. Voice stays recognizable; tone can become more reassuring, direct, or technical as the visitor moves through a page.

## Review the real page

Test the brief on a headline, a service paragraph, an error message, and a button. If reviewers cannot agree, the rule needs an example.

## Keep the handoff useful

A designer can use the brief while arranging content, while your content owner can use it to approve copy without turning every sentence into a committee debate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
