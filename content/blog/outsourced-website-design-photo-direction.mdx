# A Photo Direction Note for Outsourced Website Design

Describe the subject, setting, human action, crop needs, and feeling. Note where the image appears and what information the surrounding copy already provides.

## Make the decision visible

Avoid generic scenes that suggest facts the page cannot support. An image should clarify context or create a credible sense of place without pretending to document a specific result.

## Review the real page

Check the crop at every planned breakpoint, write meaningful alternative text, and keep a record of ownership or permission for supplied images.

## Keep the handoff useful

The note gives an outsourced team useful creative constraints while keeping image choices aligned with the page message and the company’s real materials.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
