## Frequently asked questions

### Can the design team approve its own exception?

No. The delivery team can document DNS cutover and rollback, propose a correction, and repeat the test. The infrastructure owner accepts residual risk or names another accountable decision maker.

### Is one screenshot enough evidence?

No. The record must preserve the route, input or state, environment, expected result, observed result, and repeatable retest instructions.
