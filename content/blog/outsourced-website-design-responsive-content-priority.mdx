# Setting Responsive Content Priorities for an Outsourced Website

For every page, identify the information that must remain visible, the information that can move lower, and the controls that must stay easy to operate. This makes mobile decisions explicit in the design brief.

## Rank the page content

Mark each block as essential, supporting, optional, or removable. Explain the reason for the ranking so the designer can preserve intent when space becomes limited.

## Review real interactions

Inspect headings, forms, tables, media, and navigation at narrow widths. A page is not responsive merely because it fits; visitors must still be able to complete its main task.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
