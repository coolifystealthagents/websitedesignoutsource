# How to Brief a Feature Comparison Page for Outsourced Website Design

Define the features in language your audience understands, then state the evidence or limitation for each one. Keep the comparison categories parallel so readers can scan them.

## Make the decision visible

Do not imply differences that the source material cannot support. If two options share a capability, say so; if a detail is unknown, send it for confirmation.

## Review the real page

Test the structure on a phone and with a keyboard. Important labels and qualifiers should not disappear inside a visual shortcut.

## Keep the handoff useful

A precise brief lets the design partner focus on hierarchy and readability while your team remains responsible for the accuracy of the comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
