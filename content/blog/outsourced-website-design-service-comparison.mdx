# Structuring a Service Comparison for Outsourced Website Design

Define the problem each service addresses, the work it includes, the evidence available, and the situation where another option is a better fit. Keep the headings parallel.

## Make the decision visible

Use plain distinctions instead of inflated superlatives. If the visitor needs a conversation to choose, say what information they should bring to that conversation.

## Review the real page

Check the comparison in a linear mobile order and verify every link or action leads to the promised detail. Review the copy with the person who owns each service description.

## Keep the handoff useful

The design partner can then emphasize meaningful differences while your team retains control of accurate service language and useful next steps.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
