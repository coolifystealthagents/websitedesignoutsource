## Why this control matters

Durable deployment checklist evidence needs a stable design-file or ticket reference, because a screenshot of the staging site will not prove what the page looked like last month. At Website Design Outsource, the deployment checklist record keeps the dated source and the reviewer initials for the 2026-11-02 batch. The design queue owner opens the oldest blocked page first, because age is the signal that turns a routine revision into a launch delay.
