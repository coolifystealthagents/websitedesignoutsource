# Hand Off Video Captions as Maintained Website Content

**Published: September 7, 2026**

Captions are not a switch added after a video is embedded. They are timed content that needs an approved source, review, publication, correction, and localization workflow.

## Inventory every video version

Record the page, media identifier, language, duration, transcript source, caption file, audio-description decision, poster image, player, and owner. Match captions to the exact edit so timing does not drift after the media changes.

## Set review criteria

Check spoken words, speaker changes, meaningful sounds, punctuation, timing, reading speed, and terminology. Automated transcription can accelerate a draft, but a person familiar with the content should review the delivered experience.

## Include the player experience

- Captions can be enabled and identified by language.
- Controls work by keyboard and remain visible at zoom.
- Autoplay and sound follow the approved policy.
- A transcript is easy to find when provided.
- Failure or blocked playback has an alternative route.

## Keep files editable

Deliver the caption source, final timed file, transcript, glossary, review notes, and media version in a client-owned location. Name who updates them when the video changes.
