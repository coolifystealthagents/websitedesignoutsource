# A Website Structure Review Before Outsourced Design Signoff

Trace the route from entry to answer to next step. Note repeated sections, missing context, buried proof, and any place where the visitor must remember information from another page.

## Make the decision visible

Keep observations tied to a task or audience situation. “I prefer this layout” is less useful than “a visitor cannot tell which service fits this need.”

## Review the real page

Review the page at desktop width, phone width, and in a linear reading order. Include navigation, headings, links, forms, and footer routes.

## Keep the handoff useful

A structured review gives an external design team actionable changes and gives the owner a calm basis for approving the page architecture.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
