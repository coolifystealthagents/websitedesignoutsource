# Review Website Performance With the Design Team

Performance review works best when the design team can see which page choice caused an issue. A score without the page context rarely tells anyone what to change.

## Name the experience

Record the page task, important content, test conditions, and observed behavior. Include whether the issue concerns loading, movement, input response, or content stability.

## Inspect the design choices

Review image dimensions, font loading, motion, embeds, and above-the-fold content. Ask which choice is required for the page job and which can be simplified.

## Keep the result actionable

Write the finding, evidence, owner, and next test. Do not ask the designer to solve a server or content problem without naming the person who can change it.

## Recheck after content changes

The final page may behave differently from a mockup. Review again when approved assets and copy are present.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should performance review happen only at launch?

No. Early checks can prevent a design decision from becoming expensive to change later.

### What if the cause is unknown?

Record the evidence and assign investigation. Do not turn an unresolved cause into a guess about the design.
