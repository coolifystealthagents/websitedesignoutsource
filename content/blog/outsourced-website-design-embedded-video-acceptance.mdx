# Review an Embedded Video Beyond the Play Button

**Published: September 8, 2026**

A video embed can look finished while captions are wrong, keyboard controls disappear, or a third party loads before consent. Review the media, player, and surrounding page as one experience.

## Hand over approved media details

Provide the final video, title, poster image, language, caption file, transcript, and publication owner. Say whether audio description or another alternative is required. Captions need a content review; their presence alone does not prove accuracy.

## Set player expectations

Require visible keyboard-operable controls for play, pause, volume, captions, and full screen where supported. Avoid unexpected autoplay with sound. Define how the player behaves at narrow widths, high zoom, and when a person enlarges captions.

## Decide how third parties load

Document the approved host, cookies or requests it introduces, and any consent requirement. A privacy placeholder should state what will load and keep enough space to prevent the page from jumping. Provide a direct alternative when the provider is unavailable.

## Review real states

- Before play, playing, paused, ended, and replay.
- Captions on and off at several text sizes.
- Keyboard focus inside and outside the player.
- Slow loading and blocked third-party scripts.
- Portrait phones and full-screen orientation changes.
- Transcript access without starting the video.

Record the player version, media version, route, browser, and result. If the provider changes its interface, schedule a targeted recheck rather than assuming the accepted behavior will remain fixed.
