# Include Print Styles in the Website Delivery Handoff

**Published: September 11, 2026**

Visitors still print confirmations, policies, instructions, and records. A small print specification can prevent clipped content and pages filled with navigation.

## Choose printable journeys

Name the routes and states that matter in print. Define whether navigation, forms, videos, and promotional panels disappear, and whether URLs or timestamps need to remain visible.

## Protect information order

Preserve headings, tables, captions, warnings, signatures, and identifiers. Specify page-break behavior for grouped content and avoid relying on background color to carry meaning.

## Review real output

Use browser preview and a saved PDF at common paper sizes. Check margins, overflow, blank pages, link destinations, contrast, repeated headers, and confidential content.
