Coverage for QA signoff queue during vacation or turnover depends on whether the written design procedure, not only one designer, holds the page and brand context. At Website Design Outsource, the QA signoff queue record keeps the dated source and the reviewer initials for the 2026-11-02 batch. A missing content document or alt-text list is recorded as missing, not invented from the layout, and the next message names the exact asset and the deadline.
