# A Stakeholder Review Agenda for an Outsourced Website

Send the routes, objectives, open questions, and decision owners before the meeting. Group discussion by page task or risk rather than reviewing every screen with equal weight.

## Capture useful feedback

Each comment should identify location, expected behavior, reason, and severity. Separate a required correction from a preference so the team can sequence work responsibly.

## Close the loop

End with accepted decisions, unresolved questions, owners, and dates. Update the source brief and design record together so the next review starts from the same truth.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
