# Build a Landing Page Message Map Before Outsourcing Design

Design cannot rescue an unclear proposition. A message map gives an outsourced website design team the content logic behind a landing page before visual decisions make the page harder to change.

## Start with the audience need

Describe the situation that brings the visitor to the page and the outcome they want. Use language the audience recognizes rather than internal service labels.

## Connect promise to evidence

For each important claim, name the evidence that supports it. Evidence may be a method, example, qualification, or explanation that the company can stand behind.

## Answer likely objections

List the questions that could stop a visitor from continuing and decide where each answer belongs. Do not invent testimonials or unsupported results to fill a gap.

## Define the next action

Choose a clear next step and state what the visitor should understand before taking it. Review the message map with the final page, not only the first draft.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should the message map contain final copy?

It can include working language, but its main purpose is to show relationships among need, promise, evidence, objections, and action.

### Who approves claims?

The company’s subject and marketing owners should approve claims before the design team treats them as final content.
