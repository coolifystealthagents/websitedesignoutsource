# Image Crop Specifications for an Outsourced Website

For each image, record its purpose, focal point, safe area, aspect ratios, alternative text, and acceptable crop behavior. The specification should explain what must never be cut away.

## Separate meaning from decoration

Describe the information the image contributes. If an image is decorative, say so. If it carries meaning, ensure the surrounding copy does not depend on a crop that disappears on mobile.

## Review at actual sizes

Inspect the image in the page component at each supported width. Check sharpness, loading behavior, contrast, and whether the focal point still supports the adjacent copy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
