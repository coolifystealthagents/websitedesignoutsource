# Audience Scenarios for an Outsourced Website Design Brief

Write one scenario for each important visitor: what they know, what they need to decide, and what could stop them. This gives an outsourced design team a practical basis for page hierarchy and content.

## Scenario cards

Include the visitor context, entry page, question, desired action, and evidence that would resolve uncertainty. Keep assumptions labeled so the brief does not present guesses as research.

## Review against page structure

Compare each scenario with the proposed navigation, page copy, and mobile layout. Remove pages that serve no scenario, then note any unanswered need before approval.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why include this in an outsourced design brief?

It gives the design team a concrete decision context and gives the company a useful way to review the result.

### What should be checked before approval?

Check the source record, rendered page, links, responsive behavior, and the facts or decisions that the page presents.
