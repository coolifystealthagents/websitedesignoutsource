# A Webfont Handoff Checklist for Outsourced Website Design

List each family, weight, style, file format, license owner, and intended role. Include the fallback stack and the text samples that reveal meaningful differences in wrapping.

## Make the decision visible

Check headings, navigation, buttons, forms, and long paragraphs at narrow widths. A font choice is a layout choice when it changes hierarchy or pushes an action below the fold.

## Review the real page

Keep loading behavior and readable fallback states in the review. Do not approve a beautiful desktop specimen while the real page flashes, clips, or becomes hard to scan.

## Keep the handoff useful

The checklist helps an outsourced team implement the approved type system and helps your reviewer catch licensing or responsive issues before handoff.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
