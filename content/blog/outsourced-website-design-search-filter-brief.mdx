# A Search and Filter Brief for Outsourced Website Design

List the collection fields, useful filter groups, sort behavior, no-result message, and reset path. State which choices may be combined and how the active choices are shown.

## Make the decision visible

Do not add filters merely because the data contains a field. Every filter should help a real visitor narrow the collection without creating confusing combinations.

## Review the real page

Test a first search, a refined search, clearing one choice, clearing all choices, and returning to a result. Include keyboard focus and narrow-screen behavior.

## Keep the handoff useful

With these decisions recorded, the design team can make a useful browsing experience without guessing at the content model or the recovery states.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the brief include?

Include the visitor, page purpose, approved evidence, required states, owner, and the decision the reviewer must make.

### Who should approve the design?

Name one person inside the company who can approve the content, page behavior, and final handoff.
