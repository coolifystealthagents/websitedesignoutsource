# Website Content Ownership in an Outsourced Handoff

Content handoff risk usually comes from unclear ownership rather than missing copy. A useful record names the source, editor, approver, publication location, and rollback path for every important page.

## Ownership map

| Asset | Preparing role | Approving role | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Page copy | Writer or editor | Business owner | Approved source file |
| Images | Designer | Rights owner | License and alt text note |
| Metadata | SEO editor | Site owner | URL and preview record |
| Publication | Delivery team | Company release owner | Commit and release note |

## Methodology

This article synthesizes Google Search Central, W3C, Schema.org, NIST, OWASP, Git, and the sitemap protocol. The only numeric statements are counts defined by primary standards. The ownership model is an operational recommendation for outsourced website work, not a legal determination.

## Key Stats

- WCAG 2.2 has 4 principles (W3C)
- Schema.org identifies Article as a type in its vocabulary (Schema.org)
- A sitemap urlset contains URL entries for a site (Sitemaps.org)

## Key Takeaways

- Keep the business approval decision inside the company.
- Store rights, source, and publication evidence with the asset.
- Separate an editorial correction from a production release decision.

## Evidence sections

### Copy and source evidence

Keep the approved copy, source URL or research note, date checked, and editor in one record. Google recommends useful, descriptive content and links. That guidance makes a clear editorial owner more valuable than a large, unreviewed content queue.

### Images and accessibility evidence

An image record should include origin, license status, intended use, dimensions, and alternative text decision. W3C explains that text alternatives must serve the purpose of the image in context. A decorative image and an informative image should not receive the same treatment.

### Publication evidence

The final record should link the approved source, changed file, reviewer, commit, and release owner. Keep production credentials under company control and use least privilege for contributors. A handoff is complete when the company can understand and change the asset without reopening an external conversation.

## Consolidated statistics

The article relies on 4 WCAG principles and two standards describing structured content and URL sets. These counts explain the review frame; they do not estimate traffic, rankings, or editorial output.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who approves outsourced copy?

The company should name the business owner who approves claims, tone, and publication. The delivery team can edit and recommend changes.

### What belongs in an image handoff?

Keep the original or source link, license note, crop decision, alt text decision, and the page where the asset is used.
