# Cookie Consent Design in Outsourced Website Projects

Consent design is a product and legal-interface concern, not a decorative banner task. An outsourced team can make the interface inspectable by mapping each control to the purpose, technology, and user choice it represents.

## Research basis

The ePrivacy Directive, GDPR guidance, and UK ICO guidance describe different legal contexts, so a design review should establish the audience and jurisdiction before proposing behavior. The review should also distinguish strictly necessary storage from optional measurement or advertising storage.

## Handoff questions

Ask what loads before consent, which purposes are presented, whether refusal is as prominent as acceptance, how preferences are revisited, and how the implementation records the choice. Keep legal interpretation with the client and counsel; the design partner should expose observable interface behavior.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a design partner choose the legal basis?

No. The client and its advisers own that decision. The partner can document the interface and implementation assumptions that follow from it.

### Is a single accept button always sufficient?

Not as a general rule. Requirements depend on jurisdiction and purpose, so the review should test the available choices and their prominence against the applicable guidance.
