# Preview Link Exposure Study

**Published: October 22, 2026**

## Research question and decision boundary

Preview Link Exposure Study asks how long preview links remain usable and whether access is revoked after review. The unit is one issued preview link. At stage 1, the investigator records link ID, scope, recipient, expiry, access count, revocation, and content classification under WDO22-R-preview-link-exposure-study-1. Eligibility, exclusions, versions, and stopping rules are declared before observations are interpreted. The outsourced team may assemble the fixture and capture results, but the security owner retains product, accessibility, privacy, security, legal, and release decisions. A result describes the declared implementation and observation window; it is not a universal benchmark or certification.

At research question and decision boundary, the primary source and supporting authorities define terms and possible mechanisms, yet they do not prove that a particular delivered page behaves correctly. The protocol therefore keeps raw observations beside the coded result, records unavailable states instead of treating them as passes, and asks a second reviewer to repeat the highest-consequence condition. For valid, expired, revoked, and forwarded states, disagreements remain visible with reviewer, timestamp, tool or browser version, route, fixture, expected state, observed state, and resolution owner. Counts are not pooled across incompatible environments.

During research question and decision boundary, the analysis tests an alternative explanation before accepting the most convenient narrative. Network variability, cached state, browser policy, assistive-technology behavior, extensions, content changes, and instrumentation can each alter the observation. The reviewer changes one declared factor at a time, restores the baseline, and documents whether the result persists. Unsupported inference is separated from directly observed evidence. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does this protocol certify the delivered website?

No. It produces bounded, repeatable evidence for the declared implementation and leaves policy, legal, accessibility, security, and release conclusions with accountable client owners.

### How should conflicting observations be handled?

Preserve both records, check the fixture and versions, repeat the high-consequence state, and publish the unresolved limitation rather than averaging disagreement away.
