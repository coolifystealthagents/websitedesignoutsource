# Content Density Reviews for Outsourced Website Design

A polished layout can still be difficult to scan when hierarchy, line length, spacing, and content priority are unclear. Content-density research helps a client review design decisions against the reading task rather than personal taste.

## Research basis

WCAG text-spacing and reflow guidance establish user-facing constraints, while the U.S. Web Design System and plain-language guidance emphasize clear hierarchy and scannability. An outsourced review should test representative real copy, not only empty layout blocks.

## Review record

For each template, note the primary task, heading order, paragraph length, list use, line measure, whitespace intent, and mobile behavior. Record which content is essential, supporting, or removable so revisions preserve meaning while reducing friction.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is more whitespace always better?

No. Whitespace should clarify relationships and priority. Excessive separation can make related content appear unrelated.

### Should the designer edit the copy?

The content owner should approve substantive edits. The designer can identify density problems and propose layout or editorial questions.
