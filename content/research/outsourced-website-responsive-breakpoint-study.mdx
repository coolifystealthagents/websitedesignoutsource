# Responsive Breakpoint Reviews for Outsourced Websites

Responsive quality is about preserving content relationships as the viewport changes, not about matching a short list of device screenshots. A handoff review should examine layout rules, readable measures, overflow, and interaction at widths between the supplied references.

## Research basis

CSS media queries and container queries support conditional layout, while WCAG reflow guidance focuses on avoiding loss of content or two-dimensional scrolling for ordinary page content. These standards suggest testing the system's transitions, not just named devices.

## Evidence to request

Capture representative routes at narrow, medium, and wide widths, then record changes in navigation, columns, tables, images, forms, and focus order. Ask the design partner to identify intentional exceptions and the rule that governs each one.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many device widths should be tested?

Choose widths that represent the audience and layout transitions, then include intermediate widths to expose brittle assumptions. A fixed device count is not a quality metric.

### Should the designer supply breakpoint values?

They should supply the intended behavior and constraints. Implementation may choose values that preserve those constraints in the actual content.
