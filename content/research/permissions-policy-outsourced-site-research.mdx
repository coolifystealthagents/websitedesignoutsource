# Permissions Policy Research for Outsourced Websites

**Published: October 12, 2026**

## Research question and unit

Permissions Policy Research for Outsourced Websites asks which browser permissions evidence a client should require from one identified component and release candidate. During research question and unit, record the standard version, browser or tool version, route, fixture, observed state, expected state, reviewer, exception, and owner under WD-R-permissions-policy-outsourced-site-research-1. The observation is qualitative and bounded to that implementation.

For research question and unit, a second reviewer repeats the highest-impact browser permissions evidence state and records the result under WD-R-permissions-policy-outsourced-site-research-1, retaining disagreements instead of averaging them away. The cited specifications inform this checkpoint but cannot certify the delivered site, establish legal compliance, prove accessibility, or supply a universal pass threshold. ### Does this research certify the site?

No. It defines repeatable evidence and explicit inference limits.

### Who accepts an exception?

The client names an accountable product, accessibility, privacy, security, or engineering owner.
