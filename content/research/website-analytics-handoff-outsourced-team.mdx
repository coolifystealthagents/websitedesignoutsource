# Analytics Handoff Requirements for an Outsourced Website Team

Define the business question before naming an event. For each event record its trigger, parameters, destination, consent rule, expected count, and owner. Avoid collecting data that the team cannot explain or use.

## Operating model

Test in a non-production environment with known actions. Record browser, route, event payload, consent state, and expected result, then repeat the check after release changes.
The company should own the analytics property and access list. The delivery team can implement and test events, but approval of data use and retention stays with the company.

## Review record

A useful record names the owner, scope, evidence, acceptance condition, and next action. Keep the record with the route or component change so a later reviewer can understand the decision without reopening every conversation.
Use small, repeatable checks and preserve failed as well as passed results. This makes the work easier to compare across pages and gives the company a reliable basis for approval.

## Key Takeaways

- Name an owner and acceptance condition before production starts.
- Preserve evidence with the change record.
- Escalate access, privacy, security, and launch risk to the company owner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who owns the final approval?

The company should name the decision owner. The outsourced team can prepare, implement, and test the work, but the company should accept scope, access, risk, and release decisions.

### What evidence belongs in the handoff?

Keep the brief, change record, test environment, findings, retest results, source files, and unresolved exceptions together.
