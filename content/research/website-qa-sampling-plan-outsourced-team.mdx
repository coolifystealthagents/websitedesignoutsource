# Website QA Sampling Plans for Outsourced Teams

When a site has many pages, a sampling plan makes limited review time explicit. Sample by template, risk, traffic or business importance, form behavior, responsive state, and recent change rather than reviewing only the easiest pages.

## Key Stats

- WCAG 2.2 has 4 principles (W3C)
- Core Web Vitals has 3 metrics (web.dev)
- HTTP status codes have 5 classes (IANA)

## Key Takeaways

- Define the sampling frame before selecting pages.
- Include high-risk workflows and changed components.
- Treat sample findings as signals, not proof that untested pages pass.

## Methodology

The plan uses official accessibility, performance, and HTTP references to define review areas. Sampling recommendations are operational guidance, not a statistical confidence claim.

## Sample frame

List routes, templates, content owners, forms, integrations, language variants, recent changes, and known exceptions. Select at least one representative page per template and add pages with high business or access risk.

## Record and escalate

For every task record route, device, browser, expected result, actual result, severity, evidence, owner, and retest. Escalate broken forms, access exposure, missing redirects, inaccessible critical tasks, and data-loss risk to the company owner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a sample prove every page works?

No. It provides evidence about selected pages and patterns. State the untested scope explicitly.

### How should a sample change after a defect?

Add the affected template, component, and nearby workflow to the next retest set.
