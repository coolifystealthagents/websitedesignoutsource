# Form Layout Accessibility in Outsourced Website Projects

Forms combine content, interaction, validation, and sensitive information. A design handoff should make the relationship between labels, controls, instructions, errors, and submission states explicit.

## Research basis

WCAG form criteria and the HTML specification provide the core semantics, while WAI-ARIA guidance describes when additional relationships are appropriate. The safest review starts with native HTML behavior and adds custom interaction only where its semantics are documented.

## Review method

Test a complete form with keyboard only, an empty submission, an invalid value, a corrected value, and a server-side failure. Record the accessible name, required state, error association, focus destination, and whether entered data remains available after correction.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are visual labels optional when placeholders exist?

Placeholders are not a reliable replacement for persistent labels. The review should verify the accessible name and the visible instruction separately.

### Who decides which fields are required?

The business and content owners decide what data is necessary. The design partner should make required state and consequences clear.
