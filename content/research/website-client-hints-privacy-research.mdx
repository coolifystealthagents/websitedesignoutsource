# Client Hints Privacy Boundaries for Outsourced Websites

**Published: October 13, 2026**

## Research question and decision boundary

Client Hints Privacy Boundaries for Outsourced Websites studies client hints collection and variation through one response path with declared requested hints and cache behavior. At stage 1, the investigator records request headers, opt-in lifetime, variation, necessity, and retention under WD13-R-website-client-hints-privacy-research-1. Eligibility, exclusions, versions, and stopping rules are declared before observations are interpreted. The outsourced team may assemble the fixture and capture results, but the client retains product, accessibility, privacy, security, legal, and release decisions. A result describes the declared implementation and observation window; it is not a universal benchmark or certification.

At research question and decision boundary, the primary source and supporting authorities define terms and possible mechanisms, yet they do not prove that a particular delivered page behaves correctly. The protocol therefore keeps raw observations beside the coded result, records unavailable states instead of treating them as passes, and asks a second reviewer to repeat the highest-consequence condition. For client hints collection and variation, disagreements remain visible with reviewer, timestamp, tool or browser version, route, fixture, expected state, observed state, and resolution owner. Counts are not pooled across incompatible environments.

During research question and decision boundary, the analysis of client hints collection and variation tests an alternative explanation before accepting the most convenient narrative. Network variability, cached state, browser policy, assistive-technology behavior, extensions, content changes, and instrumentation can each alter the observation. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does this protocol certify the delivered website?

No. It produces bounded, repeatable evidence for the declared implementation and leaves policy, legal, accessibility, security, and release conclusions with accountable client owners.

### How should conflicting observations be handled?

Preserve both records, check the fixture and versions, repeat the high-consequence state, and publish the unresolved limitation rather than averaging disagreement away.
