# Image Alt Text Handoffs for Outsourced Websites

Alt text is a content decision attached to an image's purpose. A design handoff should tell the content owner whether an image is informative, decorative, functional, or a link, then preserve that decision in the rendered HTML.

## Research basis

WCAG text-alternative criteria and HTML image semantics distinguish meaningful alternatives from decorative images. WAI guidance also warns against repeating nearby text or describing irrelevant visual detail.

## Handoff record

For each asset, record its role, proposed alternative, nearby caption or link text, and the final `alt` behavior. Review hero images, icons, diagrams, charts, and linked thumbnails as separate cases because their user task differs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should decorative images have descriptive alt text?

Usually not. A decorative image should be implemented so assistive technology can skip it, while nearby meaningful content remains available.

### Is the designer always responsible for alt text?

No. Responsibility should be assigned in the handoff, and the content owner should approve meaning-sensitive descriptions.
