# A Website Analytics Implementation Brief

Analytics work starts with decisions, not tags. The brief should explain what the team needs to learn, what data is necessary, and how consent and access will be handled.

## Define the measurement contract

Document event name, trigger, parameters, owner, destination, retention, consent behavior, and test evidence. Keep business definitions separate from vendor-specific implementation so the measurement survives a tool change.

## Methodology

This note synthesizes official analytics, privacy, and tag-management guidance. It is not legal advice and does not determine a jurisdiction’s consent obligations.

## Key Stats

- Google Analytics 4 uses events as its measurement model (Google)
- NIST Privacy Framework has five core functions (NIST)

## Key Takeaways

- Define the question before the event.
- Minimize personal data and document access.
- Test consent, firing, payload, and reporting behavior.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an implementer decide what to track?

They can recommend events, but the business owner should approve purpose, access, and retention.

### Is an event firing proof that reporting is correct?

No. Validate the payload, processing, dimensions, and resulting report.
