# Contrast Audits for Outsourced Website Design

Color decisions travel through brand files, components, templates, and content. An outsourced design review is more reliable when contrast is checked at the point where a visual decision becomes a reusable interface rule.

## Research basis

WCAG 2.2 defines contrast requirements for text and non-text content, while its relative-luminance model explains why perceived brand color alone cannot establish compliance. The practical implication is to review text, controls, focus indicators, and meaningful graphics against their actual backgrounds.

## A useful review record

For every finding, record the foreground and background tokens, rendered state, affected component, measured ratio, applicable success criterion, and proposed adjustment. This gives the client a durable decision record and lets the design partner retest the same state after revision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should a logo be judged like body text?

No. Apply the criterion that matches the content type and state, and document exceptions rather than treating every brand mark as ordinary text.

### Who approves a brand-color change?

The client should approve brand-impacting changes; the design partner should provide measured alternatives and affected-component evidence.
